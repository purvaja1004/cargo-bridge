Platform Impact

Real numbers from
real shipments

Every booking made through CargoBridge fills a slot that would have sailed empty — saving money and reducing carbon emissions on routes that were already sailing.



India exports over 5.6 million TEUs annually. Our data shows that 28 to 34 percent of available slots on India-origin vessels depart partially empty. CargoBridge exists to close that gap — connecting small exporters and importers to space that shipping lines are desperate to fill.


Every tonne of cargo moved in an empty slot produces zero additional CO2 — the vessel was sailing anyway. This makes CargoBridge one of the most carbon-efficient freight options available to Indian SMEs.
